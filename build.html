The build

Five nodes, left until they die.

This is the whole project until a dead node can say why it stopped. Places are not chosen. Boards are not bought.

One person is paying for the travel and the parts. There is no funder. The nodes stay out as a stress test. The thing I come back for is the diary in flash: voltage, time awake, and why a node last restarted.

What each one is

  • N01 reads the river.
  • N02 reads it again. Both have to agree.
  • N03 is a radio on a ridge. No sensor.
  • N04 keeps the log. A laptop is enough while someone is there. After that, the flash on the node is the record.
  • N05 is the end of the valley. A buzzer for the test. Not a siren left alone.

What has to be true

  • Power: LiFePO4 and a small panel. A power bank is a weekend, not a season.
  • Place: above the flood, sensor looking at the water.
  • Box: gasket and screws. Not potted. A dead node that cannot be opened teaches nothing.
  • Diary: the last heartbeats stay in flash. Voltage, time awake, why it last restarted.

Before they are left

  • A bucket at N01 has to reach N05 with no phone in the path.
  • Pull the battery on N02. The frame still arrives, the long way.
  • A note on the box, in the language spoken there: what it is, that it is not a bomb, and how to reach me.
  • A siren only if a person who sleeps near it asks for the sound.

How the radio is meant to work

The packets ride on MeshCore. Companion, repeater, and sensor roles already exist in that project. Which of those roles matches N01 through N05 is still open. The corridor drawn on the protocol page is our draft of the flood path, not a MeshCore map and not a survey.

The parts list and the repair rules, which are rules and not a description of a built box, are on hardware.

Self-funded. Five nodes. Not a deployment.
