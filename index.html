Built on MeshCore

The radio layer is MeshCore, the open LoRa mesh from meshcore-dev. UpstreamMesh is the flood sensing and the household warning we are building on top of it. We do not maintain MeshCore, and this project is not part of theirs.

Their site is meshcore.io. Firmware for supported radios is flashed from meshcore.io/flasher. The library is released under the MIT license.
